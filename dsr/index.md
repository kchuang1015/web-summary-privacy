# Data Subject Requests (WholeTake Cloud)

> This document is not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer; it was written from public law, Paddle's requirements and the published practice of similar products, and it describes how the service actually works. Retention periods, allowances and prices are those shown on the pricing page.

This process covers only the data kept by WholeTake Cloud, run by the operator (see "Operator" in the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/)). Data the Extension keeps on your device is under your control — delete, clear or export it in the Extension; the operator has no access to it.

## Two channels

| Channel | How | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-service** (recommended) | Sign in to the account page on the website, or the web app | Downloading, deleting or editing your own data — takes effect immediately |
| **Manual** | Email support@marlubie.com from your account's email address | Requests self-service cannot handle, objections, requests made through a representative |

## Identity check and response times

- **Identity check**: before acting on an emailed request we confirm that it comes from the account holder: write from your account's email address, or reply to the confirmation we send to it. We don't ask for identity documents unless there is no other way to confirm.
- **Response times**: we reply to requests for access or a copy within 15 days of receiving them, and to other requests within one month and no later than 30 days.
- **Extensions**: if we need longer, we tell you why and when to expect an answer before the deadline, within the extension the law allows (once by 15 or 30 days under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, Article 13; up to two further months under GDPR Article 12(3)). For California residents the CCPA period is 45 days.

## When we may refuse a request

If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive (for example, repetitive), we may refuse it or charge a reasonable fee and will explain why; the same applies where the law lets us withhold information (for example to protect other people's rights). When we refuse, we tell you which authority you can complain to.

## How to exercise each right

| Right | Self-service | Manual |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** (what we hold about you) | "Download full backup" on the account page: every record, the concept library, topic pages and attachments in your cloud copy, in the same format as the Extension's knowledge-graph backup, importable straight into the Extension; the same backup also includes server data kept under your account (community, consent records, keyed hashes of URLs sent to cloud processing — we do not keep the URLs themselves and a hash cannot be read back into a URL; if you give us a URL, we can tell you whether it is among them); the account page also lists devices, plan and usage | Ask by email for account data that is not in the backup (ledger, task records, security-event audit records, notification settings); after account deletion, a deletion record in backup storage can only be matched by account ID; we cannot find it by email address (GDPR Article 11(2)) |
| **Correction** | Edit a record's title, content (notes) and URL, and its tags or concepts, in the web app | Corrections to topic pages (which you can also edit in the Extension and sync), device names and account data such as the email address |
| **Erasure** | Delete single items in the web app; delete the account on the account page (the data the account owns is deleted with it, except the following, kept under [Data retention](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/retention/): the audit records, the reports you filed (with the reporter field cleared), the trial identifier, the content fingerprints of removed community content, the Paddle payment event records without an account ID, the auto-renewal consent records, and deletion records in the operator's off-site backup storage (a one-way hash of the account ID and the deletion date, used to delete the account again after a restore from backup; see the "Backups" row in Data retention)) | Deletion requests when you cannot sign in; requests to empty only the cloud copy while keeping the account |
| **Portability** | The same full backup as "Access" (machine-readable JSON) | — |
| **Restriction / objection** | Turn off sync, stop sharing and revoke MCP authorizations (the web app's "Sharing and AI assistants" page), disable automation rules, unfollow feeds, turn off notifications, unpublish — each independently | Explain any other objection by email; if you object to processing based on legitimate interests, we assess your case and reply. For an objection to comparing your published or cited URLs with the URLs you sent to cloud processing (task records or keyed URL hashes): normally we explain why we keep it (the server-side check is the only one that cannot be bypassed and it protects third parties' rights); if you show a particular situation (for example, you hold the rights to the content at those URLs), we delete those records and hashes for your account and tell you that the server will no longer block those URLs |
| **Withdrawing consent** | As above: consent to each WholeTake Cloud feature can be withdrawn separately; withdrawal does not affect processing already done | — |

## Community content

- You can **unpublish** a knowledge package you published to the community directory at any time (one edition or the whole package), and make a public knowledge graph / topic page private again at any time.
- **Copies other people have already downloaded cannot be recalled**: unpublishing or deleting your account removes it from the directory and the server files are deleted under the retention policy, but copies others already downloaded and imported into their own knowledge graphs are beyond the operator's reach. The license-confirmation screen says so before you publish.
- A suggested correction of yours that the author merged becomes part of the author's edition; after you delete your account, the directory shows the attribution as "deleted account".
- If you believe content someone else published infringes your rights, press "Report" on that item in the directory or email support@marlubie.com with the item's link, which rights are affected and why, and how to reach you. We review reports without undue delay and tell the author what we decided and why; the author can appeal a takedown within the appeal window (30 days, technical constant). Content removed under a court or authority order, or a legal notice, is handled as that order or notice requires, and the edition is deleted from our servers at once (copies in backups disappear when the backups rotate out under the retention policy).

## Outside this process

- Payment and invoice data is kept by Paddle (merchant of record); please contact Paddle — the operator will help route the request.
- Upstream AI model providers: the operator does not keep request content; requests on the provider side follow its policy (see [Subprocessors](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/subprocessors/)).
