# Data Retention Policy (WholeTake Cloud)

> This document is not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer; it was written from public law, Paddle's requirements and the published practice of similar products, and it describes how the service actually works. Retention periods, allowances and prices are those shown on the pricing page.
> Retention days, quotas and prices are **not written here**: they live only in the commercial settings file (catalog `retention`), which is what the pricing page shows. Numbers marked "technical constant" are server engineering settings; they can change, and this document is updated with them.

This document describes what WholeTake Cloud keeps on its servers, for how long, and how it is deleted. Data the Extension keeps on your device (recent results, knowledge graph, settings) is managed by you; see section 3 of the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/). This document covers only the service run by the operator.

## Principles

1. **The cloud copy is a copy of your data**: the knowledge records, concept library and topic pages uploaded when sync is on, and the results of cloud tasks, are stored in your account's cloud copy. Each of your devices keeps its own local data.
2. **Kept while your plan is active; read-only afterwards, then deleted**: when a paid plan or a plan we gave you ends (once trials open, a trial as well), the cloud copy becomes read-only for a retention period — the pricing page has the number of days. Trials are not currently offered; once they open, an account that only used a trial gets its own retention period, also on the pricing page. A reminder email is sent before the end; **nothing is deleted unless the reminder was sent** (the deletion date is the one written in that email). If the plan is restored during the retention period, the cloud copy becomes writable again.
3. **Deleting the account deletes everything the account owns**: every database table owned by the account is deleted with it; file storage (attachments, page snapshots, exports, community files) is deleted by a scheduled job that removes the account's whole prefix and retries until done. Only the items marked "kept after account deletion" below remain (security-event and moderation audit records, reports with the reporter field cleared, the trial identifier, the content fingerprints recorded when a knowledge-package edition is taken down or removed for legal reasons, or when a public knowledge graph / topic page is removed for legal reasons, Paddle payment event records without an account ID, auto-renewal consent records, and deletion records in the operator's off-site backup storage, which hold only a one-way hash of the account ID and the deletion date or time); they contain no page content, and a daily job deletes each one when its period ends (the rows below give each period and when it starts). The daily trial count per email domain, and the rate-limit counters keyed by an IP address or a keyed hash, are not data the account owns (they are not linked to an account), so account deletion leaves them; each is cleared at the period in the table below. Rate-limit counters keyed by the account ID are deleted with the account.
4. **Where content is not needed, it is not stored**: AI request content and output are not stored; server logs, the usage ledger and notification delivery records contain no page content, URLs, titles, prompts or output. **Cloud-task records are the exception**: they hold the task's URL, final URL, title and a content hash (no page text) — see the table; the keyed URL hashes kept after task records are cleared are not the URLs themselves — also in the table.
5. **Everything has an end date**: every row below says how long it is kept (the one exception is a record VicTodo keeps in a rare case, described in its row); where it says "period on the pricing page", the period is set in the commercial settings file and shown on the pricing page.
