# Subprocessors (WholeTake Cloud)

> This document is not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer; it was written from public law, Paddle's requirements and the published practice of similar products, and it describes how the service actually works. Retention periods, allowances and prices are those shown on the pricing page.
> Each provider's training, retention and processing-location details are taken from its published terms and help pages (checked on the date in each row); where a published page doesn't say, we write "not stated publicly".

These service providers handle your data only when you use WholeTake Cloud (you sign in and pick it as the AI service, turn on sync, or use the web app, the community or cloud tasks). The your-own-key (BYOK) and Chrome built-in AI paths involve none of them: on the BYOK path your content goes straight to the AI endpoint you configured, which handles it under its own terms.

Operator: see "Operator" in the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/).

## Not subprocessors, disclosed for completeness

- **The operator's own AI gateway**: WholeTake Cloud's model calls go by default through an OpenAI-compatible gateway the operator built and runs, which forwards them to Anthropic or OpenAI. It is a relay run by the operator, **not a third-party aggregator** (no OpenRouter-style third party). The gateway runs on the operator's own server in Taiwan, and WholeTake Cloud's servers reach it through Cloudflare Tunnel; it only relays and does not record request or response content (its logs for WholeTake keep metadata only; confirmed by the operator on 2026-11-04). So the content sent to a model passes through Taiwan before it reaches the upstream model provider; see "International transfers" in the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/).
- **Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation)**: only after you turn on "Allow public knowledge-base lookup (Wikidata)" in the Extension does the Extension send concept names directly to www.wikidata.org (not through the operator's servers); multilingual label enrichment for the public concept registry is optional and off by default, and when on the server sends only Wikidata Q-IDs. Wikidata does not process data on the operator's behalf, so it is not listed as a subprocessor.
- **The same operator's VicTodo server**: the operator runs WholeTake and another product, VicTodo, from the same Paddle account, so Paddle also sends WholeTake's payment event notifications (for example Paddle's customer and subscription IDs, and the WholeTake account ID we attach) to VicTodo's server. VicTodo is the operator's own product, not a third party engaged by the operator, so it is not listed as a subprocessor. Its server code runs on Cloudflare Workers placed in Australia (Sydney, AWS ap-southeast-2), and it stores the event ID, type, the time it occurred and the time it was received (not the notification's contents) in its Supabase database in Australia (Sydney, AWS ap-southeast-2); these records are also in VicTodo's daily off-site backups, which are stored in Cloudflare R2's Western Europe region (WEUR). VicTodo's off-site backups are encrypted, and only a key kept offline can open them (on a day when encryption fails, that day's copy is not encrypted and VicTodo alerts its admin); the backups made by its database provider, Supabase, are encrypted by Supabase. For when VicTodo also reads WholeTake subscriptions or transactions from Paddle (it does not keep what it reads), see "Account and sign-in" in the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/); for how long these records are kept, see [Data retention](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/retention/).
- **Google (Sign in with Google)**: used only if you choose to sign in with Google. You authenticate on Google's sign-in page, and Google then gives us your email address, Google account identifier, name, profile picture URL and tokens (what we keep and for how long: "Account and sign-in" in the [privacy policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/) and the [data retention policy](https://wholetake.marlubie.com/privacy/retention/)). Here Google is a sign-in service you use yourself; it processes the data from your Google sign-in under its own privacy policy and is not the operator's subprocessor.
- **The AI endpoint you chose yourself (BYOK)**: you deal with that provider directly; it is not the operator's subprocessor.
- **An MCP AI assistant you authorize**: you authorize it in the web app and can revoke it at any time; it handles what it receives under its own provider's policy and is not engaged by the operator.

## Change notice

When we add or replace a subprocessor, we update this list and email WholeTake account holders at least 30 days in advance; if we must replace one urgently (for example, because it stops its service or after a security incident), we tell you as soon as we can and explain why. If you object, you can turn off the features involved, or end your subscription and we refund the unused part of the current period pro rata.
